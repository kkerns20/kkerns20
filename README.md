# Kurt Kerns

Process & Business Systems Analyst building toward Solutions Engineering through hands-on infrastructure, automation, data, and integration projects.

My work sits at the intersection of **business processes, systems, data, and people**. I focus on understanding how a process works, identifying constraints, translating requirements into practical solutions, and communicating those solutions clearly to technical and non-technical audiences.

- **Home infrastructure:** Linux, Docker, networking, storage, VPNs, monitoring, and troubleshooting
- **Integration:** APIs, HTTP/JSON, Python, automation, and system-to-system workflows
- **Data:** SQL, Power BI, Excel, analysis, and turning findings into recommendations
- **Cloud & automation:** building toward AWS/Azure fundamentals, infrastructure as code, and CI/CD
